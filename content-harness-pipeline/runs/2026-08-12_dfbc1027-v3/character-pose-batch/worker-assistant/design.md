# Worker Assistant Design Lock

## Identity invariants

- Adult male construction assistant, 7–7.5 heads tall, lean build and narrower shoulders than the lead worker.
- Long oval face, light golden-tan skin, thin arched eyebrows, round black eyes, small round nose, and softly full curved lips.
- Short slightly curly black hair; a small fringe remains visible beneath the helmet.

## Style invariants

- Match the inspected Baek Seung-yong educational character references: clean polished 2D illustration, warm medium-dark brown contour, simple rounded shapes, restrained facial acting.
- Bright upper-left daylight, two to three value steps, one restrained highlight, no texture noise.
- Full-body isolated figure with slim, balanced margins.

## Outfit invariants

- Plain white matte safety helmet with no badge or lettering.
- Plum long-sleeve work shirt under a dark forest-green safety vest.
- Warm gray-brown work trousers and black work shoes.
- Fully opaque body and clothing; no gloves, tools, or added accessories.

## Alpha/background rules

- Final output must be a transparent PNG.
- Generate against a perfectly flat removable chroma key; no ground plane, cast shadow, reflection, or environmental object.

## Negative constraints

- Do not copy the reference worker's yellow helmet, blue shirt, white gloves, broad build, face, or hairstyle.
- No text, speech bubble, watermark, background, photorealism, 3D render, flat icon style, exaggerated anime emotion, cropped feet, malformed hands, or semi-transparent clothing.

## Pose compatibility

- Keep the entire helmet and both shoes in frame.
- Hands and facial silhouette must remain readable at HTML character scale.
- Concerned pose should face screen-right without changing identity, proportions, outfit, or rendering.
