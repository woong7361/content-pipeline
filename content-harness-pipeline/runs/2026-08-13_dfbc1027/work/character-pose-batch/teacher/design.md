# 여 교사 Design

## Source Of Truth

- Character id: `teacher`
- Identity source: planner `characters[].identity` and `identity_context`
- Style references: `source/baek-seungyong/assets/characters/teacher-idle.webp`, `teacher-praising.webp`
- Usage target: shape-hunt, shape-count, activity-one-bridge, free-drawing

## Identity Invariants

- Adult woman, 7 to 7.5 heads tall, natural straight build.
- Soft oval face, light beige skin, large deep amber oval eyes, small round nose, gently arched thin brows.
- One small beauty mark on her left cheek.
- Dark black-brown long hair in one low ponytail, soft side part on the right side of her forehead.

## Outfit Invariants

- Light lavender collared cardigan.
- Dark teal calf-length pleated skirt.
- Beige flats and a silver round wristwatch.
- No props.

## Style Invariants

- Match the observed Baek Seung-yong adult character style: medium dark-brown contour, clean rounded silhouette, large oval eyes with one or two white highlights, restrained blush and mouth.
- Two or three flat shading steps, narrow highlight accents, clear sunny top light, no realistic reflection.
- Do not copy the reference teacher's bob haircut, mint blouse, cream trousers, face, or identity.

## Alpha And Canvas Rules

- Final output: transparent PNG.
- Full body visible with head and feet uncut and generous margin.
- Character, hair, skin, clothing, shoes, and watch fully opaque.
- No floor, scenery, cast shadow, text, or watermark.

## Negative Constraints

- No photorealism, SD proportions, exaggerated expression, malformed fingers, extra objects, background, text, or UI.
- Never change hairstyle, beauty mark, outfit colors, skin tone, proportions, or watch between poses.

## Pose Compatibility Notes

- Idle faces front and fits left-side placement.
- Explaining pose stands on screen-left; open hand and gaze point to screen-right with clear transparent space ahead of fingertips.
